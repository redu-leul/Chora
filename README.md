#  CHORA

CHORA is an Ethiopian-centered wellness and body awareness application that helps users understand what their body is trying to communicate through simple physical, emotional, and mental signals.

It is designed for children, teenagers, adults, and elderly users with different experiences tailored to each group.


##  Problem

Many people in Ethiopia and similar contexts:
- Lack simple health education
- Misunderstand body signals (pain, stress, fatigue)
- Have limited access to clear emotional and wellness guidance
- Do not have tools that fit local lifestyle and culture



##  Solution

CHORA acts as a body signal translator, helping users understand their health in a simple and friendly way instead of medical complexity.

It focuses on:
- Mental health awareness
- Emotional wellbeing
- Nutrition understanding
- Body signal interpretation
- Elder wisdom preservation



##  User Modes

###  Child Mode
- Body learning games
- Food and health interaction
- Simple pain identification

###  Teenager Mode
- Emotional wellbeing support
- Stress and lifestyle awareness
- Body signal explanations

###  Adult Mode
- Health awareness guidance
- Family wellbeing understanding
- Lifestyle-based health insights

###  Elderly Mode
- Voice-friendly support (concept)
- Medication reminders
- Life wisdom sharing
- Emotional care tools



##  Features

- Body Signal Translator (core feature)
- Age-based user experience
- Child interactive learning
- Emotional check-ins
- Elder wisdom archive (concept/demo)
- Ethiopian lifestyle-focused health awareness


##  Tech Stack
